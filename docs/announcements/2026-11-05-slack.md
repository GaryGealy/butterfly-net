:butterfly: *Butterfly Net is live, and you can try it*

My desk is perpetually covered with 3×5 note cards, and I've been trying to find a way to cut down the clutter and stop buying stacks of cards. So I built Butterfly Net: those same cards, digital, for catching thoughts before they flutter away, at your desk or on your phone. Type or dictate a thought, AI turns it into a card (title, pile, notes, subtasks), you confirm, and it sits on its pile until you mark it done.

It went live on Cloudflare 18 days after the first commit, and I never spent more than an hour on it in any one day: at most 18 hours of my time to production. Five weeks in, it's had 14 releases, with 211 tests (140 unit + 71 Playwright) running in CI.

*How I built it with AI*
• Claude Code with the Superpowers workflow: brainstorm → spec → 15-task TDD plan, with me making the calls
• Impeccable to audit and sharpen the UI (design system, accessibility, layouts that hold up at 200% text)
• Every change goes through a branch, a PR and CI; production deploys only on a `v*` tag

*AI is in it*
• Claude Haiku sorts each thought using structured output (a JSON schema, checked again with Zod)
• 5 s timeout and 20 sorts a day; if the AI is slow or you're out of sorts, you pick the pile yourself

*Stack:* SvelteKit + Svelte 5, Tailwind 4, Cloudflare Workers + D1 (SQLite) via Drizzle, Anthropic SDK, Vitest, Playwright, GitHub Actions

*Try it:* sign up with your teamsparq.com email → https://butterfly-net.gary-m-gealy.workers.dev/welcome
The page says invite-only; your company email is your invite. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. Feedback welcome!
