## What it does

Thoughts are like butterflies: if you don't catch them, they flutter away.
Butterfly Net catches them.

It's a digital stack of 3×5 cards. Say or type a thought, like "um, remind me to
grab batteries for the smoke detectors next time I'm at the hardware store," and
Claude turns it into a card: a short title ("Pick up batteries for smoke
detectors"), the right pile (*house*), and the extra detail in notes ("Next time
at the hardware store"). You glance at the preview, press Enter, and the card
sits on its pile until you mark it done.

- **Capture is one motion.** A hotkey opens a bare capture page. Speak with
  macOS Dictation or type, then press Enter.
- **Claude does the sorting, you have the final say.** Every card shows as a
  preview first. Change the title, the pile, the notes or the subtasks, or
  accept a new pile Claude suggests. Nothing saves until you confirm.
- **Built for rambling.** Dictated thoughts come out run-on and full of "um" and
  "so I was thinking." Butterfly Net cuts them down to a short to-do and moves
  the details into notes, without making anything up.
- **Piles, not projects.** Your cards are grouped by pile. It starts you with
  *house*, *work stuff*, *ideas*, *video games* and *model building*; add, rename
  or reorder piles as you like.
- **Done means tossed, not lost.** Finished cards leave their pile but stay
  searchable in the archive, and you can reopen any of them.
- **Never loses a thought.** If Claude is slow or unavailable, your words still
  show up in the preview as typed, ready to file by hand.
- **Works like an app.** Install it from the browser for its own window and Dock
  icon on the Mac, or on your phone.

### What it deliberately doesn't do

No due dates, reminders, priorities or recurring tasks. Butterfly Net is for the
someday-things you don't want to forget, not a calendar or a project manager.
Leaving those out keeps capture faster than reaching for a paper card.

## Who it's for

People who think faster than they file: anyone whose desk has piles of index
cards, sticky notes or a junk-drawer notes app. It's ideal for:

- someone who gets ideas in the middle of something else and needs to get them
  down in seconds
- hobbyists with several pursuits (house projects, models, games) whose to-dos
  get mixed together
- people who would rather talk than type

## Positioning

**For** people who lose good thoughts because writing them down is a chore,
**Butterfly Net** is a capture tool that turns a spoken or typed thought into a
sorted card in seconds. **Unlike** to-do apps that ask you to pick a project, a
date and a priority before you can save, Butterfly Net asks for nothing: Claude
files the card and you confirm with one keypress.

**30 seconds.** Most of what we want to remember isn't urgent. It's "fix the

## Messaging guidelines

- Lead with the feeling ("catch the thought before it's gone"), then the speed
  ("one hotkey, one keypress").
- Say "Claude sorts it, you confirm." Never suggest it saves or acts without
  you.
- Don't promise reminders, due dates, offline capture, sharing or integrations.
  v0.1.0 has none of them.
- Voice works through the device's own dictation (macOS Dictation on the Mac).
  Don't describe it as a built-in voice assistant.
- Use real cards from the product as examples. Don't invent user counts,
  testimonials or reviews.
